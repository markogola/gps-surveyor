Everything you need in the field

Built for surveyors, engineers and field teams who need positions they can trust, and data they can take straight into their tools.

Live coordinates

Decimal, DMS, degrees-minutes or UTM, with accuracy, sea-level altitude, speed and heading.

Averaged waypoints

Hold still and the app combines dozens of fixes into one point, typically within 1–3 m outdoors.

Interval route recording

Collect a point every 1 second to 10 minutes. Smoothing removes jitter; recording continues with the screen off. Watch how it works.

Street and topo maps

See routes and waypoints on OpenStreetMap or a topographic map with contour lines.

Satellite detail

Satellites in use by system (GPS, Galileo, GLONASS, BeiDou) and signal strength, so you know how good a fix is.

Route statistics

Distance, duration, average speed, altitude range and climb, plus every recorded point.

Export to the tools you already use

Share by WhatsApp, email, Drive or Bluetooth, or save the file straight to your phone.

Private by design

  • No account and no sign-in
  • No ads and no tracking
  • Your locations stay on your phone
  • Files leave only when you choose to share or save them
Positions come from your phone's GPS and are estimates. GPS Surveyor is a field tool, not a certified survey instrument; see the Terms of Service. Read how data is handled in the Privacy Policy.
